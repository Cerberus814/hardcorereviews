The most beautiful roommates are back! Five Hearts Under One Roof season2.

📅 Aug 1, 2026👤 BY CERBERUS814
Live Action FMV Dating Sim
👍
RECOMMENDED 17.2 hrs on record
Five Hearts Under One Roof Season 2 keeps the FMV dating sim formula, but with some important changes. Sadly, part of the original cast is gone. Not all the girls return, but Mal-sook, Gran Dong, and Min-jung are back and feel stronger than ever, with better writing, more screen time, and more natural performances.
Five Hearts Under One Roof 2 Screenshot.by Cerberus814
The biggest improvement is the story structure. Routes are more complete, with more choices, more branching paths, and many more endings. Each girl has multiple outcomes, including hidden endings, and you can learn more about their past, which adds more depth compared to the first game.
Five Hearts Under One Roof 2 Screenshot.by Cerberus814
Production quality is still great, with good visuals and real locations. The tone mixes romance, drama, and humor, including funny scenes and memes inspired by internet and pop culture that hit right away. Gameplay is simple, but the expanded content and better routes make this a clear upgrade.
