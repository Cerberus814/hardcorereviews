Beautiful Cyberpunk Tower Defense! Beacon of Neyda.

📅 Aug 4, 2026👤 BY CERBERUS814
Tower Defense TD Pixelart
👍
RECOMMENDED 1.3 hrs on record
Beacon of Neyda is a cyberpunk tower defense with a strong identity. Set in a post apocalyptic world where machines rebel against humanity, the story feels very inspired by Terminator, which works well and gives context to the action.
Beacon of Neyda screenshot.by Cerberus814
Gameplay mixes classic tower defense with active control. You build towers, barricades, and energy networks, but you also move your character, shoot enemies, and collect resources. There are different energy types, drones, and upgrades, making the game deeper than it looks at first.
Beacon of Neyda screenshot.by Cerberus814
In short the pixelart is one of the best parts. Animations, colors, and overall atmosphere are excellent. Sound and music also help a lot with immersion. It can be challenging and takes time to learn, but it’s very rewarding. A great indie game that deserves more attention.
