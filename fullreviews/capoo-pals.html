Capoo Pals is a very colorful, cute and cozy desktop companion game where you interact with adorable Bugcats using your mouse while you are working or navigating on the web. You can move them around your desktop, watch them perform funny animations with silly sounds, and let them do different activities, feed them and clean their poop but never forget to pet them! You can unlock new Capoo Pals by combining them over time, resulting in new pets with themed skins. There are 109 Bugcats to collect in total, including DLC exclusive ones, and completing the full collection can take a while, but that’s part of the fun!

So… what are Bugcats? 🐱
Bugcats are the little blue cats you’ll see running, jumping, and fooling around on your desktop.
Capoo Pals Screenshot.by Cerberus814
The game features 5 DLC packs with fully themed and exclusive Bugcats, which I honestly recommend if you want to collect them all. Spring Pack and Summer Pack add 18 Bugcats each, The Arrival of King Capoo adds 7 exclusive Bugcats, Autumn Pack adds 18 new Bugcats, including my personal favorite: Zombie Bugcat! which looks insane and last but not least the Winter Pack adds the final 18 Bugcats. All the new Bugcats designs are very creative and cute.

🐧 The game only runs on Windows. Sadly, I couldn’t get it working on my Linux setup.

SOME COOL STUFF I FOUND!

The music sounds really good but gets a bit annoying over time. Here is how you can add your own chill or cozy music in just a few steps, you will need a ".wav" file, name it "background_music.wav" and move it to YourSteamFolder\steamapps\common\CapooPals\data\flutter_assets\assets\sounds and DONE!.
In addition you can modify the game time to get new Bugcats faster, just modify the "Game_Time.json" file, you can find it on YourSteamFolder\steamapps\common\CapooPals\data\flutter_assets\assets\json, find the id '16' change the value "120" to a lower number like for example "15" or "30".
Finally in the same "json" folder, you can play with the file "Game_Parameter.json" to change Bugcat behavior.
