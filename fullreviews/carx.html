*Most Wanted* drifting game on steam! CarX Drift Racing Online.

📅 Aug 3, 2026👤 BY CERBERUS814
Racing Drift Multiplayer
👍
RECOMMENDED 15.0 hrs on record
CarX Drift Racing Online is a racing game focused on drifting, with a mix of arcade feel and realistic physics. It’s easy to get into, but it takes time to really control the car and master the drift. The handling feels smooth and satisfying once you get used to it.
Carx Screenshot.by Cerberus814
One of the best parts is the tuning. You can change almost everything: visuals, parts, and driving setup. If you don’t know how to tune, you can download setups from the workshop, which makes the game much more accessible. There are also many cars and tracks to try. The Steam workshop also includes custom liveries, body kits, cars and tracks.

Carx Screenshot.by Cerberus814
🏁 It’s a love letter to anyone who grew up with PS2-era classics like Underground, Most Wanted, Juiced 2, and Midnight Club...

The game includes several modes like drift, time attack, and multiplayer. It also has replay, photo mode, and active community content. It’s not perfect, but it’s very fun and offers a lot of content. If you like drifting, this is an easy recommendation.
