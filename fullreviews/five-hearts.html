I Had to Buy Her the Tightest Stockings… Five Hearts Under One Roof.

📅 Feb 18, 2026👤 BY CERBERUS814
Live Action FMV Full Motion Video Dating Sim
👍
RECOMMENDED 14.8 hrs on record (4.0 hrs at review time)
This fantastic game and more on Hardcore Reviews! ⚡

Five Hearts Under One Roof is a romantic FMV story that went viral thanks to its stunning cast of beauties and charming characters. You get to know five very different love interests, the childhood friend, first love, an idol, your boss (She is the final boss), and the younger girl... each brought to life with memorable performances.
Five Hearts Under One Roof Screenshot.by Cerberus814
Fantastic production and direction by Storytaco, with impressive image quality and interesting real world locations that enrich the story. The game offers a huge amount of scenes tied to meaningful choices, letting you freely jump back through a dynamic story tree to explore alternate paths and endings. Great menu design, featuring exclusive and hidden scenes, achievements, and player voting to pick a favorite girl.

Five Hearts Under One Roof Screenshot.by Cerberus814
