🎄A Roguelite for Christmas! Folk Hero.

📅 Dec 21, 2025👤 BY CERBERUS814
Roguelike Pixelart Exploration
👍
RECOMMENDED 3.8 hrs on record
Check this game and more on Hardcore Reviews! ⚡

Folk Hero is an addictive rogue-lite hack’n slash with beautiful, minimalistic pixel art graphics and extremely fun, engaging gameplay. It’s set in a medieval fantasy world with a unique atmosphere thanks to its fantastic level design and great attention to detail. The game also supports up to 3 players in co-op, making each run even more enjoyable with friends.
Folk Hero Screenshot.by Cerberus814
🐧 It runs natively on Linux. I had an old save from when I played it on Windows, and thanks to Steam Cloud I was able to continue right where I left off, wonderful!
Folk Hero Screenshot.by Cerberus814
Exploration in Folk Hero is very pleasant and combat feels great. Each level is randomly generated and ends with a powerful boss. It’s a classic Zelda style rogue-lite focused on clearing rooms, completing side quests, discovering villages with NPCs, shops to buy upgrades or potions, and facing a wide variety of enemies trying to end your run.

Folk Hero Screenshot.by Cerberus814
In short, Folk Hero is an extremely fun and addictive game. Some steam users may describe it as a bit too difficult, but personally I find it to be a very well-balanced challenge, of course shaped by the RNG of each run. It’s a must play for fans of the genre, and in my opinion, an underrated gem that deserves more recognition. A great pick during Steam sales, perfect to put on the tree.
