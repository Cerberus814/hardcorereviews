What a gem of a game! Momodora: Reverie Under The Moonlight

📅 Jul 23, 2026👤 BY CERBERUS814
Anime Metroidvania Pixelart 2D 2D Platformer
👍
RECOMMENDED 9.8 hrs on record

Momodora: Reverie Under The Moonlight is a classic metroidvania with beautiful pixel art and a very clean visual style. The environments, animations, and overall presentation are simple but really well done, making it easy to enjoy every area you explore.

Momodora Screenshot.by Cerberus814

Gameplay is fast and responsive. You have melee attacks, a bow for ranged combat, and some special abilities that can help a lot in tough situations. Exploration feels good, with secrets, upgrades, and backtracking just like you expect from the genre. Difficulty can be quite high, especially on hard mode.

Momodora Screenshot.by Cerberus814

FOR SPECIAL ATTACK USE ⬆️⬆️⬇️⬇️⬅️➡️ ⚠️ You can only perform one fairy special attack at a time; to use another fairy special attack, you need to visit a prayer point.

The game is not very long, but it’s a solid experience from start to finish. Good music, nice level design, and fun boss fights make it worth it. If you like metroidvanias, this one is an easy recommendation.
