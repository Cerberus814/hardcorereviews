Build the momentum!!. SomnaBuster.

📅 Jan 4, 2026👤 BY CERBERUS814
2D Platformer 2D Pixelart Indie Game
👍
RECOMMENDED 0.9 hrs on record
Check this fantastic game and more on Hardcore Reviews! ⚡

SomnaBuster is another banger from one of my favorite indie developers, PD_CGT. It’s an extremely fun and challenging platformer that shares the beautiful and very unique cartoonish anime-style seen in Disaster Arms and Cycle Chaser, alongside an amazing soundtrack and a cool webcomic-style story full of adorable characters.
Somnabuster Screenshot.by Cerberus814
In addition it shines thanks to its solid and super fun gameplay.. offering a wide variety of polished mechanics such as gravity inversion, double jumps, launching yourself like a canon, and bouncing off enemies. All these elements allow the player to move through levels smoothly, building momentum and taking full advantage of each mechanic. The game constantly pushes you to improve your skills, creating the perfect environment for speedrunners.

Somnabuster Screenshot.by Cerberus814
🐧I always run my games on my Linux set-up SomnaBuster is working fantastic under GE-Proton!

In short, SomnaBuster stands out not only for its excellent artwork and OST, but also for its tight and enjoyable gameplay. It’s a classic-style platformer with linear levels and modern mechanics that come together to deliver a very rewarding experience. A must-play for fans of the genre and for any player looking to discover a hidden gem on Steam. If you want to support this awesome developer, don’t forget to wishlist It and definitely grab it during Steam sales!
