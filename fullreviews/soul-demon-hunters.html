🎅🏿 A hot cursed nun ghoul thirsty for blood? For me? Thanks, Santa!! Soul Demon Hunters.

📅 Dec 23, 2025👤 BY CERBERUS814
Roguelike Pixelart Anime 2D
👍
RECOMMENDED 7.6 hrs on record
Check this game and more on Hardcore Reviews! ⚡

Soul Demon Hunters stand out as a very fun fast paced roguelite that combines twin stick shooter action with deck building mechanics. As a demon hunter, you must survive massive enemy waves and purify six beautiful yet dangerous mythological creatures. The gameplay is very fun and challenging and each run feels very rewarding. There are a ton of weapons and combinations, level up and skill tree character progression, side quests, and shop purchases!
Soul Demon Hunters Screenshot.by Cerberus814
The game is really colorful, visually stunning with a beautiful anime-style pixel art and adorable character designs, offering a playful and charming reinterpretation of mythological beings as demon girls with their own personalities. While some sound effects could be improved, I really love the OST and I really think it perfectly complements the visuals and the gameplay style.

Soul Demon Hunters Screenshot.by Cerberus814
🐧 Soul Demon Hunters runs very well on Linux using Proton, with only occasional FPS drops when the screen is filled with enemies (On Windows It should work just fine). The developer seems very active, and I’m confident the game will keep improving over time. It’s also important to note that this is a SFW version of I Will Purify You, removing explicit adult content while keeping its fun. Overall, I really think this is a must-play for fans of the genre, and a great option to pick during the steam sales!
