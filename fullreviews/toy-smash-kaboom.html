🎄 The perfect toy story for Christmas! Toy Smash Kaboom!

📅 Dec 12, 2025👤 BY CERBERUS814
Minigames Roguelike PVP Cartoon
👍
RECOMMENDED 3.5 hrs on record (2.8 hrs at review time)
Toy Smash Kaboom! is an extremely fun tabletop backpack-like autobattler PVP game with beautiful cartoon artwork and cool, adorable characters. It offers addictive PVP gameplay where you manage your inventory or backpack, combining different toys and items to create your own builds. There are many toys and items with different stats, upgrades, and combinations, giving you tons of possibilities.
Toy Smash Kaboom! Screenshot.by Cerberus814
Even though it’s a PVP game, the battles are against other players’ builds, so you can play at your own pace with no stress. It feels great to leave a match and come back later without losing anything, while still keeping that competitive touch. RNG, inventory management, and strategy are key to victory!

Toy Smash Kaboom! Screenshot.by Cerberus814
Long story short, Toy Smash Kaboom! is the perfect game to buy, gift, and play this Christmas. It was my very first time with this kind of game, and its mechanics felt fresh, unique, and extremely fun. It has that roguelike challenge I love, plus the amazing work of the talented artists really shows, with so much love and attention to detail. It’s an absolute must play this holiday season!
